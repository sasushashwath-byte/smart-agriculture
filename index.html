Crop Information

Explore different crops and their growing requirements.

Rice

Requires warm climate, fertile soil and sufficient water.

Wheat

Grows well in cool weather with moderate irrigation.

Maize

Needs sunlight, fertile soil and proper drainage.

Cotton

Requires warm temperatures and well-drained soil.

Modern Farming Methods

🚜 Precision Farming

Uses GPS, sensors and data to improve farming efficiency.

🌿 Organic Farming

Uses natural fertilizers and eco-friendly farming practices.

🏡 Greenhouse Farming

Controls temperature and humidity for better crop growth.

💧 Hydroponics

Grows plants using nutrient-rich water without soil.

Modern Irrigation Techniques

💧 Drip Irrigation

Delivers water directly to plant roots and reduces wastage.

🌧 Sprinkler Irrigation

Distributes water over crops like natural rainfall.

🌱 Surface Irrigation

Water flows over the soil surface to reach crops.

Soil Information

Black Soil

Suitable for cotton, soybean and certain pulses.

Red Soil

Suitable for groundnut, millets and pulses with proper nutrients.

Alluvial Soil

Fertile soil suitable for rice, wheat and sugarcane.

Laterite Soil

Can support tea, coffee and cashew with proper management.
